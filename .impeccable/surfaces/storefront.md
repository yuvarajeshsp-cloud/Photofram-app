# Surface brief: storefront and customizer

Scope: `/` (home), `/frames` (catalog), `/frames/[slug]` (customizer), content pages. `/admin` takes the same type, palette and density only.

Visitor mode: Persuade for home and catalog; Operate for the customizer and admin.

Audience and job: Indian families and gift buyers, phone and desktop equally, choosing a layout, adding photos, ordering on WhatsApp.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm gift store whose one dramatic idea is the wall. Refuses the pastel product-grid store where the frame floats on a grey card; here every frame is shown hanging on a real wall.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, ink #111827 text, one blue accent #2346d8 (admin-configurable), Onest throughout. Walls are textured distemper colours (mint, sky, salmon, lime-white) with nail, string and a soft offset shadow. Rounded 12px controls, hairline #e5e7eb borders.

STORY: Visitor sees a frame on a wall, changes the wall colour, understands "my photos, my wall", taps Create your frame, designs, checks it on their wall at true size, orders on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: headline, one-line lede, primary button, WhatsApp/shipping/ready facts. Right: textured wall panel with the heart collage hung on a nail, wall-colour chips beneath it. Primary action top-left of the fold.

FORM: Owner-chosen fusion of sample D (clean modern store) with sample B's wall preview. seed: none (pinned) — "I like b and d. B is for wall preview and d is for elegant."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
